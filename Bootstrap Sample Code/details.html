La Vieille Ferme Blanc

La Vieille Ferme Blanc is a crisp, aromatic, and easy-drinking white wine from the Southern Rhône Valley, France, produced by the Famille Perrin. It is a dry, pale-yellow blend of Bourboulenc, Grenache Blanc, Ugni Blanc, and Vermentino, featuring notes of citrus, white flowers, and pear with a fresh, mineral, and zesty finish.
